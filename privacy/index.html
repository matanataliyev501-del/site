2. What do we collect?

When you send a project enquiry or an open application, we process the details you provide, such as your name, email, organization, project brief, budget range, professional experience and links you choose to share. We ask you not to submit sensitive information.

3. Why do we use it?

We use this information to respond to enquiries, consider potential cooperation and handle candidate expressions of interest. The applicable legal basis depends on the nature of the communication, including steps requested before entering into a contract, legitimate interests and, where required, consent or applicable employment law.

4. Form processing provider

Our forms use the third-party service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to transmit submitted data to our mailbox. Your data is sent to that provider when you submit a form. Review the provider’s privacy information.

5. Storage and recipients

Information is accessible to authorized personnel and service providers involved in handling messages. It is kept only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose and legal requirements. The form provider states that it retains submission archives for up to 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing and, where applicable, withdraw consent or request data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority (UODO). Contact us via email to exercise your rights.

7. Cookies and external resources

This website does not include analytics or advertising trackers. The site uses local CSS and JavaScript files. External form processing only occurs when you submit a form. Our hosting platform may process technical access logs.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this notice when our tools or processing change. Questions can be sent to hello@fortuna-apps.com.
